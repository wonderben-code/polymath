## Screen recordings (2 November 2026)

Each feature was run again by a fresh anonymous visitor with the browser's video recorder on (1280×800, Playwright under Chromium), so the footage shows exactly what a visitor sees, with real Meta intelligence and the live ledger. The full recordings (WebM, 40–190 MB) and the highlight cuts (MP4, 2–3 minutes each: the start and the result at real speed, the middle as a time-lapse) are kept outside the repository and linked from the demo-footage page; the frames and logs below are the small evidence kept here.

Two things the recordings taught: the finish toast printed a whole step prompt (fixed: first sentence only), and "Still to invent" repeated near-identical questions (fixed: one wording each). A Surprise take ended with no committed bridge, which is the honest result for that draw and was not kept. The last take, a second Dyson swarm, stopped at its brief because the public funding delegation's total was exhausted; the owner's new delegation reopens funded research.
