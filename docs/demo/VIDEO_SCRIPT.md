# Polymath demo film: narration and shot list

Three minutes, built from the live screen recordings of 2 November 2026 (see `README.md` in this folder). Two cuts exist from the same shot list and the same narration: **A, the best-bits cut**, which jumps to the strongest moment of each feature and holds on the result; and **B, the unfold cut**, where each feature runs continuously from the first keystroke to the result with speed ramps instead of jumps, so the circle, the map and the stages are seen forming while the narrator speaks. Both cuts run about 2 minutes 56 seconds. The two time columns give each block's start and end in cut A and in cut B.

Read at a natural pace (about 110 to 130 words a minute). Each block fits its segment with a little room; a short silence at the end of a block is fine.

## Recording the voiceover

1. Open the draft cut and the table above side by side.
2. Record each block as the segment plays (phone voice memo or any recorder is fine; a quiet room matters more than the microphone).
3. Send the audio file; it is laid over the picture, trimmed to the segment starts, and the final MP4 is rendered.
