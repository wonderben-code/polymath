## Current position

**Stage: The local research → conjecture/document → approval → publishing/reconciliation pipeline and scheduled source collection are implemented and fixture-verified. The three modes share the worker, atomic dispatch/budget controls, durable evidence, and owned workspace. Session-owned knowledge search/map launches evidence-traceable selected-bridge investigations; saved findings can become conjectures, summaries, and papers. Paths are locally validated with explicit fixtures; live Meta research remains disabled.**

Scope: the complete product, including Surprise Me, the knowledge base, map, and connections between connections. Meta intelligence controls research. See [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).

**First engineering objective:** A1–A5 in the build plan: establish real Meta access and capabilities, create the application foundation, and complete one bounded research investigation with real sources, a structural mapping, an objection, and an evidence-backed conclusion or honest rejection.

**Next work:** Meta-led reassessment of findings against newly collected sources, followed by safe full-text retrieval. Scheduled collection and bibliographic identity are implemented locally. Native Meta/Zenodo verification and public deployment remain external prerequisites. The three research modes, memory, and higher-level map follow-up now work through fixture-backed end-to-end tests. Actual Meta capabilities/pricing/token bounds, scientific quality, and public deployment remain unverified. Paid calls remain forbidden until the owner confirms readiness and permits them; a key appearing alone is not permission. No fixture finding is a live discovery.

Proposed initial seeds: self-organised criticality; error-correcting codes; phase transitions in learning. Include a weak/misleading input and an evidence-changing case. These are evaluation inputs, not prewritten discoveries. Confirm their suitability using the first live investigations.

Track: real source identity, quote integrity, claim support, mapping assumptions, objection quality, prior-work search coverage, useful rejection, adaptive next actions, latency, usage/cost, job recovery, and deployed completion of every feature. Confidence scores are not calibrated probabilities until evaluated as such.
